# 应用图标

图案参考来自用户本机已安装的 Slay the Spire / Slay the Spire 2 EXE 内置图标：后方红色卡牌、前方绿色卡牌，叠放后分别添加罗马数字 I / II。游戏识别图案的权利属于 Mega Crit 及相关权利人；项目代码的 MIT 许可不表示对游戏图案的再授权。本项目为非官方工具。

排版与编号编辑使用内置 image_gen 工具完成。app-icon.png 为选用的透明 PNG；app-icon-small.png 是界面用缩略图；app-icon.ico 包含 16–256 像素多个尺寸。派生文件仅进行格式及尺寸转换，保留透明通道。

最终编号编辑提示词（输入为已叠放的两代游戏卡牌图标）：

> Edit this existing two-game-card application icon. Change ONLY the addition of two Roman numeral labels; preserve every other aspect: the exact red card artwork, green card artwork, overlapping arrangement, size, rotation, original colors, shapes, transparent background, and all card borders. Put a large bold warm-gold Roman numeral 'I' centered in the lower dark blank panel of the rear red card. Put a matching large bold warm-gold Roman numeral 'II' centered in the lower dark blank panel of the front green card. Each numeral must follow the perspective and rotation of its own card, remain fully visible, and not cover the existing picture in the upper half of either card. Use simple sturdy serif Roman lettering with broad strokes and very subtle dark edging, matching the golden borders, no new plaque or badge. Numerals should fill approximately half of each lower blank panel, readable when the final icon is small. Do not add any other text or objects. Keep background genuinely transparent. Output the same single square composition with just these two numeral labels added.
