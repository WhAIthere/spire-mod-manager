# 应用图标

图案参考来自用户本机已安装的 Slay the Spire / Slay the Spire 2 EXE 内置图标：后方红色卡牌、前方绿色卡牌，叠放后分别添加罗马数字 I / II。游戏识别图案的权利属于 Mega Crit 及相关权利人；项目代码的 MIT 许可不表示对游戏图案的再授权。本项目为非官方工具。

排版与编号编辑使用内置 image_gen 工具完成。app-icon.png 为选用的透明 PNG；app-icon-small.png 是界面用缩略图；app-icon.ico 包含 16–256 像素多个尺寸。派生文件仅进行格式及尺寸转换，保留透明通道。

最终居中调整提示词（Ⅰ按红色卡牌完整宽度居中，允许被前方卡牌遮挡）：

> Make one precise alignment edit to this existing application icon. Keep both cards, all artwork, the exact composition, transparency, colors, size and rotation absolutely unchanged. Keep the gold 'II' on the front green card exactly unchanged. The ONLY edit is the position of the gold 'I' on the rear red card: move that I about 75 pixels to the RIGHT on this approximately 1254-pixel canvas, keeping its current vertical position in the lower dark panel, size, golden serif style, and tilt. The I must be horizontally centered on the red card's FULL lower panel / full original card width, including the portion hidden underneath the foreground green card. Do NOT center the I in just the exposed visible portion of the red card. A small part of the right side of the I may naturally be hidden BEHIND the front green card; that is desired and acceptable. The front card must remain on top of any overlapping part of the I. Restore the vacated pixels of the old I position to the same plain dark-red/brown panel. Do not move the I upward onto the picture, do not change the II, and do not move either card. Return only this single corrected transparent icon.
